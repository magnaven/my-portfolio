## Milestone Summary

**Key Decisions:**
- Founder lens POV framing — authentic to Ida's dual role as designer + founder/consultant
- Two explicit audience entry points (consulting vs. hiring) — meaningfully different intents and success criteria
- 3 rich case studies over quantity — depth signals judgment better than breadth
- Bold animated hero (GSAP SplitText character reveal) — animation signals craft immediately
- Single RAF loop: Lenis driven by `gsap.ticker.add()`, `autoRaf:false` — prevents double-tick jank
- Tailwind v4 `@theme` tokens in globals.css (no tailwind.config.js) — v4 convention
- `content/` as authored copy source, `data/` as structured data — clean separation of concerns
- TDD wave-0 pattern: failing stubs before implementation — enforced consistency across phases
- Cal.com popup over Calendly embed — avoids iframe sizing issues

**Issues Resolved:**
- NavBar solid-state bug on /work/[slug] — fixed with `usePathname()`-based `alwaysSolid` detection (Phase 3)
- GSAP/Lenis jsdom incompatibility — resolved with manual vitest mocks (Phase 2)
